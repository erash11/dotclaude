## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### **Q: "No meetings found"**
A: Check your date range. Try: "Show me my last 50 meetings"

### **Q: "How do I know what's already imported?"**
A: Check the tracking file or look at your Meetings folder dates

### **Q: "Can I re-import a meeting?"**
A: Yes! Just delete its ID from `.fireflies_imported.txt`

### **Q: "The note has the wrong date"**
A: Fireflies timestamps are in UTC. The note date reflects when the meeting occurred.

### **Q: "How do I organize by project?"**
A: Create subfolders in your Meetings directory and move notes there
