# Review: Issurin, *Block Periodization* (2008), Chapter 3 (Opus falsification pass, round 1)

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-09-27. Brief: `staging/review_book_prompt.md` plus `docs/ATOMIZE_BRIEF.md`.
Scope: the 12 entries in `staging/_scope_issurin_ch3.txt` (folder `staging/library/strength-and-conditioning/block-periodization--issurin/`). The Ch1-2 entries were read for duplicates and contradictions only, not graded.
Source: `Block Periodization - Issurin.pdf`. Chapter 3 (idx 87-135, printed 76-124) was extracted in full with PyMuPDF and read. Every table and figure cited by an in-scope entry was rendered and read as a page image: Tables 3.1, 3.4, 3.5, 3.7, 3.12, 3.13; Figures 3.4, 3.5, 3.7, 3.8, 3.10, 3.11 and 3.12 (3.12 zoomed).
Primary-source spot checks: I pulled the Europe PMC abstracts for three studies the chapter retells: Hooper et al. 1995, Cardinale & Stone 2006 and Margaritis et al. 2003.

## Locator convention

- Entries cite the **0-based PDF index = printed + 11**. That holds on every Ch3 page. A viewer shows printed + 12. Below, "idx N / p.M" means index N, printed page M.
- Only one entry locator is wrong: the Cardinale & Stone case is on **idx 104 / p.93**, not idx 103 / p.92. All other page locators check out.

## Global findings (apply across rows; not repeated per row)

- **G1 Unlabelled writer voice (recurring from Ch1).** Every **Example** in scope is an invented football or Baylor scenario, or a generic walk-through, with no "Inference:" prefix. The "Population caveat:" paragraphs are unlabelled too, and so are the "this is the entry most directly answering the miss-log gap" openers. Most of these are reasonable; they just need the label. The "Inference, mapped to a college football calendar" headers are correctly labelled, which is an improvement on Ch1.
- **G2 Em dashes: clean.** The only em dashes are the house-style bold section headers. The Ch1 body-prose problem did not recur.
- **G3 False "verified against page image" stamps.** Four entries (and `_book.md`) claim page-image verification for content that the page image contradicts:
  - Figure 3.7: the entry inverts the AM/PM content.
  - Table 3.7: the "3-6 days" comment belongs to the Impact row, not Restoration.
  - Figure 3.10: the figure shows expenditure falling **to** about 69%. The text says "by up to 69.3%".
  - Figure 3.11: the entry's title says the gains were "erased", but both markers stay above baseline.

  `_book.md` says all Ch3 tables and figures "matched exactly". That is false; correct it when these rows are fixed.
- **G4 The Issurin 2016 vault brief is still unlinked.** No Ch3 entry links `research-papers/issurin-2016-benefits-and-limitations-...md`. Its line 57 ("does not supply validated block durations or effect estimates for collegiate American football") and line 58 (the multi-targeted conclusion "should not be treated as settled comparative evidence") belong in When NOT for the accumulation, transmutation, realization and prolonging entries.
- **G5 Supercompensation as the taper mechanism.** The pre-competitive and realization entries both rest "Why it works" on supercompensation (idx 106 / p.95; idx 122 / p.111). That is the author's stated mechanism, so plain text is correct. Both entries should add `[[supertraining--verkhoshansky/claim--adaptive-reconstruction-vs-supercompensation]]` (staging, `contested`) to Related.
- **Confidence:** no `proven` rating is used anywhere in scope. The three case studies are correctly `untested`. The framework entries are at the `supported` ceiling. The one `contested` entry (transmutation) is mis-grounded; see its row.
