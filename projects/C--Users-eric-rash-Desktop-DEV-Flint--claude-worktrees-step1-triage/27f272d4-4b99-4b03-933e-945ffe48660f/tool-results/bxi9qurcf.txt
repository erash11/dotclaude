Reviewer: Claude Opus (independent falsification pass), 2026-09-26.
Brief: `staging/review_book_prompt.md`. Standards: `docs/ATOMIZE_BRIEF.md`.

## How the source was checked

- Every entry was compared with the full Chapter 1 text (extracted `st_full.json`, idx 12-104, read in full) and the tables and figures were checked on the page image: Table 1.1 (viewer p. 23), Table 1.2 (viewer p. 37), Table 1.4 (viewer p. 93), the strength-deficit paragraphs (viewer pp. 21-22), Greene & McMahon and the 15-120 ms passage (viewer p. 59), Figs 1.27-1.29 (viewer pp. 75-76), and the chapter opening (viewer p. 13).
- **Locator convention.** The entries' "PDF p. N" is the extractor's 0-based index: printed + 11. A normal 1-based PDF viewer (and the Read tool) shows printed page 1 on page 13, so viewer page = printed + 12. Below, "PDF" means the entries' index (printed + 11), to match the entries. The fix is a single note in `_book.md` (row 1), not 41 separate edits.
- Rule 4 (When NOT present and substantive) was checked for all 41 entries. All have one. The one that is not really a when-not is `principle--rate-of-force-development` (a "developed later" cross-reference plus an inference); see its row.
- Eric's-baseline claims were checked against `docs/method-baseline.md`. Duplicates were checked by grep against `SecondBrain/Knowledge/library` and the staging library.

## Global findings (apply to many rows)

1. **Unlabeled "Why it works" mechanisms.** About a dozen entries give a mechanism the book does not state, with no label. ATOMIZE_BRIEF rule: write "The author gives no mechanism. Inference: ...". Each case is listed in its row.
2. **Author voice.** Many entries write "Verkhoshansky states/argues" for passages that refer to Verkhoshansky in the third person ("developed ... by Verkhoshansky", printed 34; "Verkhoshansky has shown", printed 10; "(Siff, 1998)", printed 35). These passages are in Siff's voice. Correction: write "the authors" or "Verkhoshansky & Siff" unless the text itself names who is speaking.
3. **Harmonized source conflicts.** In five places the book contradicts itself and the entry quietly picks one side: the strength-deficit direction, CAR (superficial vs deep), glycogen-only supercompensation, SEC energy storage by fibre type, and the Table 1.1 "6-80" misprint. ATOMIZE_BRIEF says to record both.
