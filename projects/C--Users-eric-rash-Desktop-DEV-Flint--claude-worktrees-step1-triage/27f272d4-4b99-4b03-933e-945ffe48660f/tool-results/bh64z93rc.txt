# Review: Zatsiorsky & Kraemer, Science and Practice of Strength Training 2e, Chapters 1-2 (round 1, Opus)

Reviewer: Claude Opus (independent, falsification brief). Date: 2026-09-27.
Scope: 15 entries plus `_book.md` in `staging/library/strength-and-conditioning/science-and-practice-of-strength-training--zatsiorsky/`.

## Method and coverage

- Every check below was made against the **page image** of the 2e scan (Read tool, `pages`), never the OCR text. Pages opened: PDF 12-24 (printed 3-15) and PDF 27-55 (printed 18-46). PDF 25-26 (printed 16-17, chapter 2 opener) were not opened; no entry cites them.
- The two-factor equation was re-rendered at 400 and 900 dpi (PyMuPDF crop of PDF p. 22) to read the exponents.
- Offset printed = PDF - 9 confirmed on every page opened.
- 1st-edition check: the 1e copy (Coach Lowe PDF) was probed page by page for text length. **1e PDF pp. 8-31 have zero characters of text layer** (pp. 1, 5 also empty). Key hits elsewhere: "recall the two-factor theory of training in chapter 1" (1e PDF p. 48); "Because of the existence of the explosive strength deficit (see chapter 2...)" (1e PDF p. 54); "The rate of force development (especially the S-gradient) does not correlate with the maximal force" and "The force in exercises with reversible muscle action does not change after heavy resistance training" (1e PDF p. 42); the Zatsiorsky & Raitsin numbers "410 ± 170 N ... 11.5 ± 5.4 kg ... 560 ± 230 N ... 7.5 ± 4.7 kg" (1e PDF p. 7).
- Baseline labels were checked against `docs/method-baseline.md` line by line.
- Dedup: grepped the vault library and the staging library; read the overlapping entries named below.

Note for the fixer: the coordinator's task brief for this review also called the printed p. 27 table "elite time-to-peak-force figures". That label is wrong (see ESD row). Do not "correct" the entry back to it.
