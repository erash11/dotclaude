Key Terms,What it means,Why it matters ,Scripted Min,\"Minutes in the numbered periods, starting after Team Stretch. Thursday Recovery is not counted.\",The main measure of practice length and load. ,Transition Min,\"Breaks, transitions and call-ups inside practice (after stretch). Should equal Total Practice Min; a difference means gaps or overlaps in the script. ,Game Day (GD-x),Days until the next game: GD-1 = day before the game. Built from Game Date on the Schedule tab minus the practice date.,\"With Saturday games, Mon = GD-5 … Fri = GD-1. A Friday game shifts every label by one day. Bye weeks have no game, so they show 'Bye'.\" ,Script Type,\"Which practice script was run (Mon–Fri). Usually the same as the day. In the bye week, Tue ran the Mon script, Wed the Tue script and Thu the Wed script.\",\"Compares like with like across weeks, even when the calendar shifts.\" ,Week Type,Game or Bye. Set automatically from the Schedule tab (Opponent = BYE → Bye; everything else → Game).,Bye weeks count toward season load but are left out of week-over-week change and game-week averages. ,Period Type,\"Each period grouped by its drill name into 14 types: Pre-Practice, Team Tempo, Special Teams, Indy, Unit Teach, Perimeter, 9-on-7 / 1-on-1, 7-on-7 / Pass Rush, Scout, Team, Competition, O/D Separate, Recovery, Transition.\",Lets you trend one kind of work across the season. ,Δ (change),\"Difference in minutes against a comparison point. Negatives show in parentheses, e.g. (10).\",\"Positive = longer, negative = shorter.\" ,Wk1→Latest Δ,Latest week with data minus Week 1.,Season-long drift for a day or period. ,2.
