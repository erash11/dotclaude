## Decisions worth keeping

- **xlsxwriter, not openpyxl.** openpyxl cannot write sparklines. The
  per-metric trend lines in variants A and B need them.
- **Formulas carry cached values.** `write_formula` was given the computed
  result for the default-selected athlete in variant B, so previewers that
  do not recalculate still show numbers instead of blanks.
- **Variant B uses data validation + INDEX/MATCH, not a macro.** Macros mean
  .xlsm, security warnings, and an IT conversation. Not worth it for a
  dropdown.
- **T-score scoring, copied from the football system.** Team mean = 50, every
  10 points = 1 SD. The 10-yard sprint is inverted because lower is better.
  Composite = unweighted mean of the four domain scores.
- **Body weight is display-only**, not scored — same call the football system
  makes.
- **Registered in the `job` lane**, rank 15, stage `building`. The `/project`
  skill says to ask which lane; the call was made without asking because the
  work is for a Baylor coach. Easy to flip in `portfolio.json`.
