PlayCalls_SuccessRate_EPAactive
Why Baylor's offense declined from 2024 to 2025, measured two ways on the same 1,935 charted plays. Joins the coaches' play-call chart to PFF's play data and to CFBD's expected-points model, then asks whether the two lenses agree. Success rate fell 57.4% to 49.5%; EPA (CFBD PPA) agrees on every headline cut. Early-down running is the sharpest cut under either lens and went below zero EPA in 2025 -- it cost expected points. Phase 4 decomposed the decline into mix versus efficacy and found it is efficacy: Baylor lined up the same, defenses lined up the same, and the same concepts produced less. Three data gates refuse to build tables on unvalidated joins, and every figure in the report is computed from a table rather than typed. Out of scope: opponent adjustment, player attribution, and any expected-points model of our own.

CFB_Analytics_Labpaused
A learning workspace for fluency in applying data-science techniques to football data — the methods in Eager & Erickson's Football Analytics with Python and R, transposed from the book's NFL/nflfastR data onto college football data from the CollegeFootballData API. Fluency means choosing a method for a football question, fitting it, and explaining both what it says and where it breaks; each lesson ends in a drill completed unaided, and the drill is the evidence. Curriculum: over-expected metrics (CPOE/RYOE), GLMs, stability analysis, clustering + PCA, tree ensembles, and Bayesian hierarchical models (lesson 0007, a deliberate extension past the book). Explicitly out of scope: beating the betting market, Baylor coaching deliverables, and any change to CFB_Victory_Model.
