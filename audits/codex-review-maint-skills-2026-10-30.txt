    31	## Generic-title patterns
    32	
    33	A title is generic if it matches any of these (case-insensitive):
    34	
    35	- Empty or whitespace-only
    36	- Exactly: `Meeting`, `Call`, `Recording`, `Zoom Meeting`, `Microsoft Teams Meeting`, `Google Meet`, `Teams Meeting`, `Untitled`
    37	- A date or timestamp and **nothing else**: `2026-05-20`, `MM/DD/YYYY`, `Meeting YYYY-MM-DD`, `Jul 29, 10:49 AM`, `Mar 02, 03:32 PM`
    38	- The user's own email plus a timestamp: `eric.rash11@gmail.com - Tue, 28 Jul 2026 10:16:54 CDT - Untitled`
    39	- Just a participant count: `2 people`, `3 attendees`
    40	- Generic prefix with nothing distinctive after: `Zoom Meeting`, `Teams Meeting`, `Google Meet`, `Meeting on`, `Call with`
    41	- Length under 5 characters
    42	
    43	Anything else is curated. Leave it alone.
    44	
    45	### Not generic — do not touch
    46	
    47	**A date *prefix* followed by content is the user's own naming convention**, matching the `YYYY-MM-DD_` filenames in the vault. These are curated:
    48	
    49	`2026-01-30_BAIR_Steering_Approval` · `2025-01-30_Marc_Lewis_Texans` · `2026-05-14_Applied_Planning` · `2025-12-19_KB_1v1` · `2026-01-09_SprintAI_Meeting`
    50	
    51	A naive `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}` match flags all of them. On 2026-07-30 that over-matched 19 curated titles out of 23 flagged — renaming them would have violated *curated titles are sacred*. The test is whether **anything survives removing the date**: `2026-01-09_SprintAI_Meeting` → `SprintAI_Meeting` is a real title, so it's curated. `Jul 29, 10:49 AM` → nothing, so it's generic. Note the word `Meeting` inside an otherwise descriptive title does not make it generic.
    52	
    53	## Title construction rules
    54	
    55	Format: `<context-or-participants> — <topic>`
    56	
    57	- Under **60 characters total**, and count it — a shared prefix eats the budget fast (`Baylor Performance Clinic — ` is 28 characters on its own)
    58	- Em dash (`—`) between context and topic, not a hyphen
    59	- **Context priority:**
    60	  1. 1-on-1 (2 participants incl. user) → other person's first name (`Sarah`, or `1on1 with Sarah` if recurring)
    61	  2. Small group (3–5) → first names joined with `&` (`Eric & John & Sarah`)
    62	  3. Large group (6+) → a context label inferred from the topic: `Internal`, `Client — <name>`, `Team Sync`, `Standup`
    63	- **Topic:** distill the gist to 3–6 words. Nouns and concrete action; drop filler.
    64	- **Forbidden words:** `meeting`, `call`, `discussion`, `sync`, `conversation`, `chat`. Exception: `1on1`, `Standup`, `Team Sync` as context labels.
    65	- **No date** — filenames already carry the `YYYY-MM-DD_` prefix.
    66	- **Fireflies strips some punctuation.** A `/` is dropped silently: `Book — Coach/Practitioner Collaboration` came back as `Book — Coach Practitioner Collaboration`. Prefer `&` or a plain space, and report the change if it happens.
    67	
    68	**Good:** `Sarah — Q2 Career Path` · `Eric & John — ForcePlate v2 Scope` · `Catapult Support — JWT Auth Issue` · `Client — Baylor Spring Football Reporting`
    69	
    70	**Bad:** `Meeting with Sarah about career stuff` (drop the verb and the filler) · `2026-05-20 Sync` (date is in the filename) · `Important discussion` (no specifics → propose `[skip — gist too vague]`)
    71	
    72	### Grouping a multi-session event
    73	
    74	When several recordings come from one event (a clinic, a conference, a webinar series), offer a shared prefix so they sort together: `<Event> — <topic>`. Ask the user what the event was rather than guessing. Budget the prefix against the 60-char cap and trim topics — dropping speaker names is usually the right trade, since they survive in the file body and action items.

    76	## CLI gotchas
    77	
    78	### `sync` must be `--full` for title edits
    79	
    80	`fireflies-pp-cli sync --agent` advances an incremental cursor and only fetches transcripts it hasn't seen. A **title edit on an already-synced transcript is invisible to it** — it exits 0 and reports `{"event":"sync_complete","resource":"transcripts","total":0}`, which reads like success.
    81	
    82	Confirmed live 2026-07-30: nine renames applied cleanly to Fireflies; plain sync reported `total: 0` twice; `transcripts find` still returned all nine *old* titles until `sync --full --resources transcripts` pulled 187 transcripts and the new titles appeared.
    83	
    84	A `total: 0` on transcripts is the tell that the cursor short-circuited. Always verify by re-running `transcripts find`, never by reading the exit code.
    85	
    86	### A stale DB hides candidates
    87	
    88	The same cursor makes step 1's `find` under-report. On 2026-07-30 the incremental DB returned 15 transcripts; a full sync returned 17, surfacing two generic-titled meetings that were never in the candidate list. This is why step 1 also uses `--full`.
    89	
    90	### `find` truncates at 100
    91	
    92	`transcripts find` defaults to `--limit 100`. Pass `--limit 100000` for any backfill. (`meeting-sync`'s `sync.py` already does this — don't remove that flag there either.)
    93	
    94	### Deleted transcripts linger in the local DB
    95	
    96	**`sync --full` does not prune transcripts deleted from the Fireflies cloud.** The local SQLite DB keeps them, so `transcripts find` lists meetings that no longer exist — ghosts. Renaming one fails with `GraphQL error: Transcript not found`, which is *literally accurate*: it really is gone.
    97	
    98	Observed 2026-07-30. Four meetings surfaced by an all-time `find` all failed to rename; the user confirmed they had been deleted from the platform and are not on the web anymore. A `sync --full` immediately afterward pulled 187 transcripts and the four ghosts were still in `find` output.
    99	
   100	**Reads cannot be trusted to confirm existence.** `transcripts get` returned complete data for all four under **both** `--data-source live` and `--data-source local`. A genuinely unknown ID returns `[]` under `live`, so the live path does execute — but for an ID with a local row, the read still produced data. Whether that's a silent local fallback or the API serving soft-deleted records is **unresolved**. Either way: a successful read does not prove a transcript still exists.
   101	
   102	**The only reliable existence signal is the write itself.** `Transcript not found` from `rename.py` means gone. Treat it as permanent and report it — no UI workaround exists, because there's nothing left to rename.
   103	
   104	**Do not infer an age rule.** The first read of this evidence was that Fireflies closes a title-edit window on old meetings (~30 days), since the four failures were 44–192 days old and 13 same-day successes were ≤20 days. That was **wrong** — age correlated only because the deleted meetings happened to be the old ones. Don't skip renames based on age.
   105	
   106	### `transcripts update` GraphQL bug — regressed, and the old fallback is unsafe
   107	
   108	Patched locally 2026-07-22 (upstream PR mvanhorn/printing-press-library#1571, **never merged**). The binary was rebuilt 2026-08-05, which wiped the local patch, and the bug was live again by 2026-08-29. Expect this to recur on every reinstall until the PR merges.
   109	
   110	⚠️ **The fallback this section used to recommend — the Fireflies MCP tool — is not safe.** It returns `success: true` without reliably writing, and delayed writes can land much later and clobber a good title. See [Why renames bypass the CLI and MCP](#why-renames-bypass-the-cli-and-mcp).
   111	
   112	Use `scripts/rename.py` instead. It does not depend on the CLI build at all, so a reinstall cannot regress it.
   113	
   114	### Harness blocks foreground sleeps
   115	
   116	Claude Code refuses foreground `sleep` / `Start-Sleep`. If the 30s inter-batch pause is blocked, do **not** chain shorter sleeps to work around it — apply the remaining batches sequentially, say so in the report, and rely on the 429 handling. Nine renames in one run stayed well inside the quota on 2026-07-30.

   118	## Why renames bypass the CLI and MCP
   119	
   120	Diagnosed 2026-08-29, after a 45-minute misdiagnosis. Both obvious paths are broken, and one of them lies about it.
   121	
   122	**`fireflies-pp-cli transcripts update` always fails.** It passes the id as a bare field argument; the schema takes one input object:
   123	
   124	```
   125	CLI sends: updateMeetingTitle(id: ..., title: ...)
   126	API wants: updateMeetingTitle(input: {id: ..., title: ...})
   127	```
   128	
   129	Every call returns `HTTP 400 ... Unknown argument "id" on field "Mutation.updateMeetingTitle"`. The field name really is `id` — a direct call using `meetingId` is rejected with `Field "UpdateMeetingTitleInput.id" of required type "String!" was not provided`. The *shape* is wrong, not the field name; do not "fix" it by renaming the field. There is no Fireflies manuscript on this machine (`~/printing-press/manuscripts` holds only catapult and zotero), so the CLI cannot currently be regenerated.
   130	
   131	**The MCP tool `fireflies_update_meeting_title` returns `success: true` without reliably writing.** Its success flag carries no information. Observed in one run: 2 of 7 renames landed promptly, 5 had not landed after 8 minutes of polling, and one diagnostic write surfaced ~20–30 minutes later. **Writes are erratically delayed, not dropped** — so a rename you believe failed can still land an hour later and clobber a good title.
   132	
   133	Two rules follow:
   134	- Never send a throwaway "test" title through MCP. Late arrival makes the probe destructive.
   135	- Never treat a mutation's own response as proof. Only a read-back proves a write.
   136	
   137	**Use `scripts/rename.py`.** It calls the API directly and verifies every write by reading the title back, so it cannot report a silent failure as success. `--check <id>` reads a live title without writing.
   138	
   139	## Rate limits and the retry ledger
   140	
   141	Fireflies documents a hard daily rate limit — but **do not reach for it as an explanation first.** The 2026-08-29 incident looked exactly like rate limiting (writes accepted, nothing persisting) and was entirely the broken MCP path above. A real rate limit surfaces as an explicit `429` / `MCP error -32429`, not as silence.
   142	
   143	- Apply in batches of 5–7 in parallel, 30s between batches
   144	- On a rate-limit error (CLI GraphQL 429, or `MCP error -32429: Rate limit exceeded`) stop immediately and persist the remaining items to `pending[]` in the pending-renames file
   145	
   146	### Transient vs permanent — classify before queuing
   147	
   148	`pending[]` is auto-applied by Resume mode without asking. That makes it **only** safe for failures that could plausibly succeed later. Putting a permanent failure there creates a loop: every future run retries it, fails, and re-queues it.
   149	
   150	| Class | Examples | Action |
   151	|---|---|---|
   152	| **Transient** | timeout, blocked call, connection reset, 5xx, `MCP error -32429` | Retry once → `pending[]` |
   153	| **Permanent** | `Transcript not found` — the transcript was deleted from Fireflies; or a title under 5 / over 256 chars | **Never queue.** Report as gone, with the reason |
   154	| **Transient** | `read-back mismatch` from `rename.py` — the write did not land | Retry once → `pending[]` |
   155	
   156	Never leave a *transient* failure with only an inline mention — a "20/22 renamed" run must end with the 2 failures persisted so Resume mode retries them. But a permanent failure belongs in the report, not the ledger. On 2026-07-30 four `Transcript not found` failures were deliberately left unqueued for exactly this reason.
   157	
   158	## Unattended mode
   159	
   160	Only for orchestrating skills that explicitly declare unattended operation (`meeting-sync` does, in its step 2). The interactive approval flow is replaced by a confidence gate:
   161	
   162	- **Apply without approval only HIGH-confidence renames.** HIGH-confidence means all of: gist retrieved and specific, participants known, title follows the construction rules with no guessing.
   163	- **Everything else** — vague gist, missing summary, ambiguous context, anything you'd hesitate to defend — goes to `review[]` as `{meetingId, current_title, proposed_title, reason}` for later interactive review via Resume mode. Do not apply it.
   164	- Report both buckets: "K applied (high confidence), R queued for review in .pending-renames.json".
   165	- Rate-limit strategy and `pending[]` persistence apply unchanged.
   166	
   167	This is the only sanctioned exception to the approval rule.

function Invoke-MeetingSync {
    param([string]$VaultDir = "/Users/kristinarash/SecondBrain/Inbox/meetings")

    Show-Section "Meeting Sync"
    $ff = Get-Command fireflies-pp-cli -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
    if (-not $ff) { Write-Host "fireflies-pp-cli not found on PATH; skipped."; return $null }

    $vaultDir = $VaultDir
    New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force -Path $vaultDir | Out-Null
    $statePath = Join-Path $vaultDir ".sync-state.json"
    if (-not (Test-Path -LiteralPath $statePath)) {
        '{"exports":{}}' | Set-Content -LiteralPath $statePath -Encoding UTF8
    }

    # A failure here used to print and return, so the cadence still logged
    # "cadence OK"; it failed silently every morning until 2026-10-23.
    # .errors.log is shown at every session start.
    $reportFailure = {
        param([string]$Message)
        Write-Host $Message
        $errLog = Join-Path $HOME ".claude/global-memory/transcripts/.errors.log"
        if (Test-Path -LiteralPath (Split-Path -Parent $errLog)) {
            Add-Content -LiteralPath $errLog -Value "$(Get-Date -Format 'yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm') keystone meeting-sync: $Message"
        }
    }

    # launchd runs pwsh -NoProfile, so the ~/.zshrc Keychain export never
    # happens. Read the same Keychain item directly (works under launchd,
    # tested 2026-10-23). Do not use `auth set-token` (name-meetings SKILL.md).
    if ($IsMacOS -and -not $env:FIREFLIES_API_KEY) {
        $key = & /usr/bin/security find-generic-password -s FIREFLIES_API_KEY -a $env:USER -w 2>$null
        if ($LASTEXITCODE -eq 0 -and $key) { $env:FIREFLIES_API_KEY = ([string]$key).Trim() }
    }

    $syncOut = @(& fireflies-pp-cli sync --agent 2>&1)
    $syncExit = $LASTEXITCODE
    $syncOut | ForEach-Object { Write-Host $_ }
    if ($syncExit -ne 0) {
        & $reportFailure "fireflies-pp-cli sync failed (exit $syncExit); skipping export this run."
        return $null
    }
    # A rejected key still exits 0; it only shows as sync_error events.
    $syncErrors = @($syncOut | Where-Object { "$_" -match '"event"\s*:\s*"sync_error"' })
    if ($syncErrors.Count -gt 0) {
        & $reportFailure "fireflies-pp-cli sync reported $($syncErrors.Count) sync_error event(s): a bad or expired FIREFLIES_API_KEY, or a Fireflies API error."
    }

    $findRaw = & fireflies-pp-cli transcripts find --from 14 --processed-only --json --agent 2>&1
    if ($LASTEXITCODE -ne 0) {
        & $reportFailure "fireflies-pp-cli transcripts find failed; skipping export this run."
        return $null
    }

    try {
        $transcripts = @(($findRaw -join "`n") | ConvertFrom-Json)
    }
    catch {
        Write-Host "Could not parse transcripts find output; skipping."
        return $null
    }

    $state = Get-Content -Raw -LiteralPath $statePath | ConvertFrom-Json
    $exportsMap = @{}
    if ($state.exports) {
        foreach ($p in $state.exports.PSObject.Properties) { $exportsMap[$p.Name] = $p.Value }
    }

    $newCount = 0
    $genericCount = 0

    foreach ($t in $transcripts) {
        $id = [string]$t.id
        if ($exportsMap.ContainsKey($id)) { continue }
        # A generic title would become a useless vault filename. Leave it unexported: the
        # meeting-sync skill (weekly-review, /fireflies-intake) renames it in Fireflies first.
        if (Test-GenericMeetingTitle $t.title) {
            $genericCount++
            Write-Host "Skipped (generic title, left for the meeting-sync skill): $($t.title) [$id]"
            continue
        }

        $before = @(Get-ChildItem -LiteralPath $vaultDir -Filter "*.md" -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue | Select-Object -ExpandProperty Name)

        & fireflies-pp-cli transcripts export $id --vault $vaultDir --agent 2>&1 | Out-Null
        if ($LASTEXITCODE -ne 0) {
            Write-Host "Export failed for $id; continuing."
            continue
        }

        $after = @(Get-ChildItem -LiteralPath $vaultDir -Filter "*.md" -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue)
        $newFiles = @($after | Where-Object { $before -notcontains $_.Name })

        if ($newFiles.Count -eq 0) {
            Write-Host "Export for $id reported success but no new file appeared in the vault; skipping to avoid tagging the wrong file."
            continue
        }
        if ($newFiles.Count -gt 1) {
            Write-Host "Export for $id produced $($newFiles.Count) new files; using the most recently written one and logging the rest."
        }
        $exportedFile = $newFiles | Sort-Object LastWriteTime -Descending | Select-Object -First 1

        $content = Get-Content -Raw -LiteralPath $exportedFile.FullName

        # Auto-tag against the workstream taxonomy. The 2026-07-24 audit found
        # project tags filled on 4 of 243 files — the section was created every
        # time and filled never, so the weekly review had to infer project
        # mapping from prose. A field that is blank 98% of the time trains you
        # to ignore it, so populate it or drop it. Tags are a starting point:
        # they are written as a normal markdown list and are meant to be edited.
        $tagLines = Get-MeetingTopicTags -Text $content
        $projectBlock = if ($tagLines.Count -gt 0) {
            ($tagLines | ForEach-Object { "- project/$_" }) -join "`n"
        } else { "- project/" }

        $header = @"
## Project tags
> Auto-tagged from ``data/meeting-topics.json``. Edit freely - add what was missed, delete what is wrong. Used by weekly-review to surface drift.

$projectBlock
- person/

## Key topics

## Decisions

## Action items
> Pulled from Fireflies. Mark complete by replacing ``[ ]`` with ``[x]``.

## Open questions

"@
        Set-Content -LiteralPath $exportedFile.FullName -Value ($header + $content) -Encoding UTF8

        $exportsMap[$id] = [pscustomobject]@{
            filename    = $exportedFile.Name
            exported_at = (Get-Date).ToString("o")
        }
        $newCount++
    }

    ([pscustomobject]@{ exports = $exportsMap }) | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10 |
        Set-Content -LiteralPath $statePath -Encoding UTF8

    Write-Host "Meetings found (last 14 days): $($transcripts.Count)"
    Write-Host "New exports: $newCount"
    if ($genericCount -gt 0) {
        Write-Host "$genericCount new meeting(s) with generic titles not exported; the meeting-sync skill renames and exports them."
    }

    return [pscustomobject]@{ NewCount = $newCount; GenericCount = $genericCount; VaultDir = $vaultDir }
}

Pipeline: doctor auth check -> fireflies-pp-cli sync -> transcripts find

All diagnostics go to stderr.

Exit codes: 0 ok, 1 unexpected, 2 usage/config, 3 sync/find failure,

Paths come from the platform-specific shared config at ../../_shared/.

def log(msg: str) -> None:

def die(code: int, msg: str) -> "None":

def load_config() -> dict:

        die(2, f"cannot read shared config {CONFIG_PATH}: {e}")

def resolve_cli(cfg: dict) -> str:
    configured = cfg.get("fireflies_cli")
    if configured and Path(configured).is_file():
        return str(configured)
    exe = shutil.which("fireflies-pp-cli")
    if not exe:
        die(2, "fireflies-pp-cli not found on PATH")
    return exe


def run_cli(exe: str, args: list, timeout: int = 300) -> subprocess.CompletedProcess:
    return subprocess.run(
        [exe] + args, capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=timeout
    )


def check_auth(exe: str) -> None:
    """Step 1: doctor. Requires JSON field "auth": "configured"
    (key comes from the FIREFLIES_API_KEY environment variable)."""
    proc = run_cli(exe, ["doctor", "--agent"])
    try:
        doc = json.loads(proc.stdout)
    except json.JSONDecodeError:
        die(4, f"doctor output was not JSON (exit {proc.returncode}): "
               f"{proc.stdout[:200]} {proc.stderr[:200]}")
    if doc.get("auth") != "configured":
        die(4, "Fireflies auth not configured. Set FIREFLIES_API_KEY in the "
               "environment (doctor reported auth="
               f"{doc.get('auth')!r}, source={doc.get('auth_source')!r}).")


def refresh_db(exe: str) -> None:
    """Step 2: pull any newly processed transcripts into the local DB."""
    proc = run_cli(exe, ["sync", "--agent"], timeout=600)
    if proc.returncode != 0:
        die(3, f"fireflies-pp-cli sync failed (exit {proc.returncode}): "
               f"{proc.stderr.strip() or proc.stdout.strip()}")


def find_transcripts(exe: str, days_back: int) -> list:
    """Step 3: list processed transcripts in the lookback window."""
    proc = run_cli(exe, [
        "transcripts", "find", "--from", str(days_back),
        "--processed-only", "--json", "--agent",
        # CLI defaults to --limit 100, which silently truncates large
        # backfills (e.g. the 235-meeting ingest); override it.
        "--limit", "100000",
    ])
    if proc.returncode != 0:
        die(3, f"transcripts find failed (exit {proc.returncode}): "
               f"{proc.stderr.strip() or proc.stdout.strip()}")
    try:
        data = json.loads(proc.stdout)
    except json.JSONDecodeError:
        die(3, f"transcripts find output was not JSON: {proc.stdout[:200]}")
    if isinstance(data, dict):
        for key in ("transcripts", "results", "items", "data"):
            if isinstance(data.get(key), list):
                data = data[key]

            die(3, f"unrecognized find JSON shape: keys={list(data)[:10]}")

def load_state(state_path: Path) -> dict:
    """Step 4 support: load {"exports": {...}}; back up and recreate if corrupt."""
    if not state_path.exists():
        return {"exports": {}}
    try:
        with open(state_path, encoding="utf-8") as f:
            state = json.load(f)
        if not isinstance(state, dict) or not isinstance(state.get("exports"), dict):
            raise ValueError("missing exports map")
        return state
    except (OSError, ValueError, json.JSONDecodeError) as e:
        bak = state_path.with_suffix(state_path.suffix + ".bak")
        shutil.copy2(state_path, bak)
        log(f"WARNING: state file corrupted ({e}); backed up to {bak.name}, "
            "starting fresh. This run may re-export (filenames are "
            "deterministic, so the filesystem stays idempotent).")
        return {"exports": {}}


def save_state(state_path: Path, state: dict) -> None:
    tmp = state_path.with_suffix(state_path.suffix + ".tmp")
    with open(tmp, "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:
        json.dump(state, f, indent=2)
    tmp.replace(state_path)


def transcript_date(t: dict) -> str:
    return str(t.get("dateString") or t.get("date") or "")


def export_new(exe: str, pending: list, vault: Path,
               state_path: Path, state: dict) -> list:
    """Step 5: export each pending transcript, detecting the written file by
    diffing the vault directory listing before/after each export (never
    "newest .md" — a no-op export must not claim an unrelated file)."""
    exported_paths = []
    for t in pending:
        tid = t["id"]
        before = {p.name for p in vault.glob("*.md")}
        proc = run_cli(exe, [
            "transcripts", "export", tid,
            "--vault", str(vault), "--agent",
        ], timeout=600)
        if proc.returncode != 0:
            log(f"WARNING: export failed for {tid} "
                f"(exit {proc.returncode}): "
                f"{proc.stderr.strip() or proc.stdout.strip()} — continuing.")
            continue
        new_files = [p for p in vault.glob("*.md") if p.name not in before]
        if not new_files:
            log(f"WARNING: export for {tid} reported success but no new file "
                "appeared in the vault; skipping state update to avoid "
                "tagging the wrong file.")
            continue
        if len(new_files) > 1:
            log(f"NOTE: export for {tid} produced {len(new_files)} new files; "
                "using the most recently written one.")
        exported = max(new_files, key=lambda p: p.stat().st_mtime)
        state["exports"][tid] = {
            "filename": exported.name,
            "exported_at": datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(),
        }
        save_state(state_path, state)  # persist per-export: crash-safe
        exported_paths.append(str(exported))
        log(f"exported {tid} -> {exported.name}")
    return exported_paths

# Each log over 2 MB keeps its last 2000 lines (the rule this script always used for its own log).
# Read into a variable first, then rewrite: piping Get-Content into Set-Content on the same file can
# truncate it before the read finishes. The rewrite keeps the same inode, and the live writers append
# with O_APPEND (lsof +fg shows AP for everythingd and this job's stdout), so their next line lands at
# the new end. shortcut: a line appended between the read and the rewrite is lost; fine for logs.
function Invoke-LogTrim([string[]]$paths) {
    foreach ($f in $paths) {
        if (-not (Test-Path -LiteralPath $f -PathType Leaf)) { continue }
        if ((Get-Item -LiteralPath $f).Length -le 2MB) { continue }
        if ($DryRun) { Log "TRIM [dry-run] would keep the last 2000 lines of $f"; continue }
        try {
            $tail = @(Get-Content -LiteralPath $f -Tail 2000 -ErrorAction Stop)
            Set-Content -LiteralPath $f -Value $tail -ErrorAction Stop
            Log "TRIM $f : kept the last 2000 lines"
        } catch { Log "TRIM $f : FAILED ($($_.Exception.Message))" }
    }
}
# Deletes top-level files older than $days, in place (launchd code never writes ~/.Trash).
# Only for folders whose files are regenerable, and only dated files (YYYY-MM-DD in the name: every
# file in both KeystoneOS folders on 2026-10-24), so a README or latest.json there is never touched.
function Remove-OldFiles([string[]]$dirs, [int]$days) {
    $cutoff = (Get-Date).AddDays(-$days)
    foreach ($d in $dirs) {
        if (-not (Test-Path -LiteralPath $d -PathType Container)) { continue }
        $old = @(Get-ChildItem -LiteralPath $d -File -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue |
            Where-Object { $_.Name -match '\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}' -and $_.LastWriteTime -lt $cutoff })
        if ($old.Count -eq 0) { continue }
        if ($DryRun) { Log "PRUNE [dry-run] would delete $($old.Count) file(s) older than $days days in $d"; continue }
        $old | Remove-Item -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
        $left = @($old | Where-Object { Test-Path -LiteralPath $_.FullName }).Count
        Log ("PRUNE $d : deleted $($old.Count - $left) file(s) older than $days days" + $(if ($left) { ", $left could not be deleted" } else { '' }))
    }
}

    31	## Generic-title patterns
    32	
    33	A title is generic if it matches any of these (case-insensitive):
    34	
    35	- Empty or whitespace-only
    36	- Exactly: `Meeting`, `Call`, `Recording`, `Zoom Meeting`, `Microsoft Teams Meeting`, `Google Meet`, `Teams Meeting`, `Untitled`
    37	- A date or timestamp and **nothing else**: `2026-05-20`, `MM/DD/YYYY`, `Meeting YYYY-MM-DD`, `Jul 29, 10:49 AM`, `Mar 02, 03:32 PM`
    38	- The user's own email plus a timestamp: `eric.rash11@gmail.com - Tue, 28 Jul 2026 10:16:54 CDT - Untitled`
    39	- Just a participant count: `2 people`, `3 attendees`
    40	- Generic prefix with nothing distinctive after: `Zoom Meeting`, `Teams Meeting`, `Google Meet`, `Meeting on`, `Call with`
    41	- Length under 5 characters
    42	
    43	Anything else is curated. Leave it alone.
    44	
    45	### Not generic — do not touch
    46	
    47	**A date *prefix* followed by content is the user's own naming convention**, matching the `YYYY-MM-DD_` filenames in the vault. These are curated:
    48	
    49	`2026-01-30_BAIR_Steering_Approval` · `2025-01-30_Marc_Lewis_Texans` · `2026-05-14_Applied_Planning` · `2025-12-19_KB_1v1` · `2026-01-09_SprintAI_Meeting`
    50	
    51	A naive `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}` match flags all of them. On 2026-07-30 that over-matched 19 curated titles out of 23 flagged — renaming them would have violated *curated titles are sacred*. The test is whether **anything survives removing the date**: `2026-01-09_SprintAI_Meeting` → `SprintAI_Meeting` is a real title, so it's curated. `Jul 29, 10:49 AM` → nothing, so it's generic. Note the word `Meeting` inside an otherwise descriptive title does not make it generic.

    53	## Title construction rules
    54	
    55	Format: `<context-or-participants> — <topic>`
    56	
    57	- Under **60 characters total**, and count it — a shared prefix eats the budget fast (`Baylor Performance Clinic — ` is 28 characters on its own)
    58	- Em dash (`—`) between context and topic, not a hyphen
    59	- **Context priority:**
    60	  1. 1-on-1 (2 participants incl. user) → other person's first name (`Sarah`, or `1on1 with Sarah` if recurring)
    61	  2. Small group (3–5) → first names joined with `&` (`Eric & John & Sarah`)
    62	  3. Large group (6+) → a context label inferred from the topic: `Internal`, `Client — <name>`, `Team Sync`, `Standup`
    63	- **Topic:** distill the gist to 3–6 words. Nouns and concrete action; drop filler.
    64	- **Forbidden words:** `meeting`, `call`, `discussion`, `sync`, `conversation`, `chat`. Exception: `1on1`, `Standup`, `Team Sync` as context labels.
    65	- **No date** — filenames already carry the `YYYY-MM-DD_` prefix.
    66	- **Fireflies strips some punctuation.** A `/` is dropped silently: `Book — Coach/Practitioner Collaboration` came back as `Book — Coach Practitioner Collaboration`. Prefer `&` or a plain space, and report the change if it happens.
    67	
    68	**Good:** `Sarah — Q2 Career Path` · `Eric & John — ForcePlate v2 Scope` · `Catapult Support — JWT Auth Issue` · `Client — Baylor Spring Football Reporting`
    69	
    70	**Bad:** `Meeting with Sarah about career stuff` (drop the verb and the filler) · `2026-05-20 Sync` (date is in the filename) · `Important discussion` (no specifics → propose `[skip — gist too vague]`)
    71	
    72	### Grouping a multi-session event
    73	
    74	When several recordings come from one event (a clinic, a conference, a webinar series), offer a shared prefix so they sort together: `<Event> — <topic>`. Ask the user what the event was rather than guessing. Budget the prefix against the 60-char cap and trim topics — dropping speaker names is usually the right trade, since they survive in the file body and action items.

    76	## CLI gotchas
    77	
    78	### `sync` must be `--full` for title edits
    79	
    80	`fireflies-pp-cli sync --agent` advances an incremental cursor and only fetches transcripts it hasn't seen. A **title edit on an already-synced transcript is invisible to it** — it exits 0 and reports `{"event":"sync_complete","resource":"transcripts","total":0}`, which reads like success.
    81	
    82	Confirmed live 2026-07-30: nine renames applied cleanly to Fireflies; plain sync reported `total: 0` twice; `transcripts find` still returned all nine *old* titles until `sync --full --resources transcripts` pulled 187 transcripts and the new titles appeared.
    83	
    84	A `total: 0` on transcripts is the tell that the cursor short-circuited. Always verify by re-running `transcripts find`, never by reading the exit code.
    85	
    86	### A stale DB hides candidates
    87	
    88	The same cursor makes step 1's `find` under-report. On 2026-07-30 the incremental DB returned 15 transcripts; a full sync returned 17, surfacing two generic-titled meetings that were never in the candidate list. This is why step 1 also uses `--full`.
    89	
    90	### `find` truncates at 100
    91	
    92	`transcripts find` defaults to `--limit 100`. Pass `--limit 100000` for any backfill. (`meeting-sync`'s `sync.py` already does this — don't remove that flag there either.)
    93	
    94	### Deleted transcripts linger in the local DB
    95	
    96	**`sync --full` does not prune transcripts deleted from the Fireflies cloud.** The local SQLite DB keeps them, so `transcripts find` lists meetings that no longer exist — ghosts. Renaming one fails with `GraphQL error: Transcript not found`, which is *literally accurate*: it really is gone.
    97	
    98	Observed 2026-07-30. Four meetings surfaced by an all-time `find` all failed to rename; the user confirmed they had been deleted from the platform and are not on the web anymore. A `sync --full` immediately afterward pulled 187 transcripts and the four ghosts were still in `find` output.
    99	
   100	**Reads cannot be trusted to confirm existence.** `transcripts get` returned complete data for all four under **both** `--data-source live` and `--data-source local`. A genuinely unknown ID returns `[]` under `live`, so the live path does execute — but for an ID with a local row, the read still produced data. Whether that's a silent local fallback or the API serving soft-deleted records is **unresolved**. Either way: a successful read does not prove a transcript still exists.

    36	## Flow
    37	
    38	### 1. Refresh and list candidates
    39	
    40	```bash
    41	fireflies-pp-cli sync --full --resources transcripts --agent
    42	fireflies-pp-cli transcripts find --from 30 --processed-only --limit 100000 --json --agent
    43	```
    44	
    45	`--full` is required: an incremental sync leaves the DB stale and `find` then under-reports candidates. `--limit 100000` overrides the CLI default of 100. See [REFERENCE.md → CLI gotchas](REFERENCE.md#cli-gotchas).
    46	
    47	Default lookback is 30 days. Adjust `--from` if the user says "last 60 days" / "all meetings" (omitting it means all-time — warn that high-volume rename is slow).
    48	
    49	### 2. Detect generics, then check they're editable
    50	
    51	Match each title against the patterns in [REFERENCE.md → Generic-title patterns](REFERENCE.md#generic-title-patterns). Skip everything else — **don't rename what the user has already curated.** A date *prefix* followed by content (`2026-01-30_BAIR_Steering_Approval`) is curated; only date-and-time-*only* titles are generic.
    52	
    53	**Expect ghosts.** `sync --full` doesn't prune transcripts deleted from the Fireflies cloud, so `find` can list meetings that no longer exist. They fail at step 5 with `Transcript not found` — literally true, and permanent. Reads don't detect them (`transcripts get` returns full data for a deleted transcript on both `live` and `local`), so you can't pre-filter; the write is the only reliable signal. Don't skip candidates by age — see [REFERENCE.md → Deleted transcripts](REFERENCE.md#deleted-transcripts-linger-in-the-local-db).
    54	
    55	### 3. Propose titles
    56	
    57	For each candidate, pull the content before proposing anything:
    58	
    59	```bash
    60	fireflies-pp-cli summary <id> --format gist --json --agent
    61	```
    62	
    63	The gist, keywords, overview, and the speaker names in `action_items` are enough to build a title. Follow [REFERENCE.md → Title construction rules](REFERENCE.md#title-construction-rules). If the gist is too vague, propose `[skip — gist too vague]` and tell the user it needs manual naming in the Fireflies UI.
    64	
    65	If several candidates come from one event, offer a shared prefix so they group — ask what the event was rather than guessing.
    66	
    67	### 4. Present batch for approval
    68	
    69	Display a markdown table with columns `# | Date | Current | Proposed | Action` (`✓` to apply, `—` to skip). Then `AskUserQuestion`: "How should I apply these renames?" with **[Approve all / Edit rows / Apply subset / Cancel]**:
    70	
    71	- **Approve all** → apply all non-skipped rows
    72	- **Edit rows** → ask which rows and what titles, confirm the revised table, then apply
    73	- **Apply subset** → ask which rows ("1,3,5" / "rows 1-2" / "skip 2,4"), apply only those
    74	- **Cancel** → exit without applying
    75	
    76	### 5. Apply approved renames
    77	
    78	```bash
    79	python3 ~/.claude/skills/name-meetings/scripts/rename.py <id> "<new title>"
    80	```
    81	
    82	**Never use `fireflies-pp-cli transcripts update`, and never use the Fireflies MCP tool `fireflies_update_meeting_title`.** Both are broken, and the MCP one lies — it returns `success: true` without reliably writing. `scripts/rename.py` calls the API directly and **verifies every write by reading the title back**, so it cannot report a silent failure as a success. Full diagnosis: [REFERENCE.md → Why renames bypass the CLI and MCP](REFERENCE.md#why-renames-bypass-the-cli-and-mcp).
    83	
    84	The script spaces its own writes and verifies each one. Run it once per meeting, or `python3 ~/.claude/skills/name-meetings/scripts/rename.py --pending` to apply a whole approved batch and prune the ledger as each one verifies. Trust its `OK` / `FAIL` lines — never a bare success flag. On a 429, stop and persist the rest to `pending[]`.
    85	
    86	**Classify every other failure before queuing it — the ledger is for transient errors only.**
    87	- *Transient* (timeout, blocked call, 5xx, connection reset) → retry once, then append to `pending[]`
    88	- *Permanent* (`Transcript not found`, or a title under 5 / over 256 characters) → **do not queue.** Resume mode auto-applies `pending[]`, so a permanent failure there retries forever and can never succeed. Report it as unrenamable, with the reason.
    89	- A `read-back mismatch` from `rename.py` is **transient** — the write did not land. Retry once, then queue it.
    90	
    91	Report each result inline. Mechanics and fallbacks: [REFERENCE.md → Rate limits and the retry ledger](REFERENCE.md#rate-limits-and-the-retry-ledger).
    92	
    93	### 6. Re-sync — must be `--full`
    94	
    95	```bash
    96	fireflies-pp-cli sync --full --resources transcripts --agent
    97	```
    98	
    99	**Plain `sync --agent` fails silently here** — it skips already-seen transcripts, so title edits never land, and it still exits 0 reporting `total: 0`. **Verify by re-running the step-1 `find` and confirming the new titles**, not by reading the exit code.
   100	
   101	If the verification `find` surfaces generics you never proposed, the earlier DB was stale — say so and offer to name them rather than ending the run silently.
   102	
   103	**Fireflies' list endpoint lags its single-transcript endpoint by minutes.** `sync` and `find` read the list; `rename.py` verifies against the single transcript. So immediately after a rename it is normal and expected for `find` to still show the old title while the rename is genuinely applied. Do **not** re-issue the rename — that is how a good title gets clobbered. Wait ~4 minutes, re-run `sync --full`, and check again. This matters most when `meeting-sync` runs next: it builds vault filenames from the synced title, so exporting too early bakes the stale title into the filename, and a later re-export creates a *second* file rather than fixing the first.
   104	
   105	**If the sync fails**, retry once. If it fails again the renames are NOT lost — Fireflies is source-of-truth and they already succeeded there; only the local DB is stale. Report explicitly: *"Renames applied in Fireflies, but the local DB re-sync failed — titles will look stale to meeting-sync/weekly-review until you run `fireflies-pp-cli sync --full --resources transcripts --agent`."* Never end with a silent DB/Fireflies mismatch.

   108	
   109	3–5 lines: N scanned / M generic · K applied · F queued in `.pending-renames.json` (next run auto-resumes) · S skipped for manual naming · re-sync status (OK, or the stale-DB warning).
